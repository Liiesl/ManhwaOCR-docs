# Image Manipulation: Stitching & Splitting

Need one long strip instead of many files — or two neat pages instead of one giant? These tools live in the **left toolbar** as **Stitch** and **Split**.

> TODO screenshot: stitching mode with selected pages + Confirm bar; splitting mode with drag line.

## Stitching Images (join pages)

Great for webtoon scenes split across files, or double-page spreads you want as one.

1.  Click **Stitch** in the left toolbar.
2.  A little bar appears at the top — you're now in Stitching Mode:
    *   Click pages in the order you want them, top to bottom. Click again to un-pick one.
3.  Pick two or more, then click **Confirm Stitch**.
4.  The picked pages become one long image. Any found text and clean-ups move along to the right spots automatically.
5.  Changed your mind? Click **Cancel** to leave without changing anything.

## Splitting Images (divide a page)

Great for chopping a long strip into panels, or a scanned spread into two pages.

1.  Click **Split** in the left toolbar.
2.  A bar appears — you're in Splitting Mode:
    *   Click the page you want to divide.
    *   Click on it where the cut should go — a draggable line appears.
    *   Drag the handle up/down to nudge it exactly where you want.
    *   **Clear** removes the line so you can pick another page.
3.  When happy, click **Confirm Split**.
4.  The page becomes two images (max two per split). Found text and clean-ups are sorted into the right new pages.
5.  To bail out, click **Cancel**.

**Tip:** Stitch before text detection when a scene flows across files — the app (and translations) get better context. Split back for export if you need separate files at the end.
